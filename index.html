STAR FLEETCOMMAND

Command a dreadnought. Lead an armada. Survive the void.

How to play
  • Select ships with left click / drag box. Shift adds. Ctrl+1–9 assigns a group, 1–9 recalls it. Tab selects the flagship.
  • Right click: move. On an enemy: attack. On an ally: escort. A + click: attack-move. S: hold position.
  • Direct Control (toggle with C or the button): WASD flies the flagship, mouse aims, left click fires the heavy cannons.
  • Flagship abilities: Q Shield Overcharge, E EMP Nova, R Spinal Lance (aim at cursor), F Launch Wing, T Micro-Jump to cursor.
  • Spend Command Points (earned over time and from kills) to warp in reinforcements.
  • Camera: edge of screen / arrow keys to pan, scroll to zoom, Space to center on flagship.
